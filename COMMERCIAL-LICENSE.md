# Revflare licensing

Revflare's source code is public, but it is not free for commercial use.

## Free: noncommercial use

Revflare (the Worker, the web app, the iOS app, the CLI, the MCP server and the skills in this repository) is
licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). You may read, run, modify and share it for
noncommercial purposes, for example:

- personal projects, learning, research and evaluation,
- charitable, educational, public research and government organizations (as defined in the license),
- trying Revflare out before buying a license.

## Paid: commercial use

Any other use needs a commercial license, in particular:

- tracking the revenue of apps that are sold or earn money (paid apps, in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads),
- use by or for a company, including agencies running Revflare for clients,
- offering Revflare, or a product built on it, to others.

A commercial license covers one self-hosted Revflare instance for a year, including updates and email support:
**[buy it on revflare.ferdi.app](https://revflare.ferdi.app/buy)** (checkout by Polar, merchant of record). Not sure
whether your use is commercial? Ask [Ferdi on GitHub](https://github.com/FerdiKT).

## Always free: the SDKs

RevflareKit for iOS ([`sdk/ios`](sdk/ios), [FerdiKT/RevflareKit](https://github.com/FerdiKT/RevflareKit)) and for
Android ([`sdk/android`](sdk/android), FerdiKT/RevflareKit-Android) ship inside apps and are licensed under the
[MIT License](sdk/ios/LICENSE): you can use them in any app, commercial or not.
